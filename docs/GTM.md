# Go-To-Market Strategy (GTM)

## Exact Target Users
- Target users are **5–20 person AI startups** using multiple subscription tools without strict procurement oversight.
- These teams typically empower engineers to expense tools directly, leading to stack redundancy (e.g., paying for Cursor, Copilot, and Claude Pro simultaneously).

## Where They Exist Online
Initial users can be acquired through:
- Indie Hackers
- r/SaaS and r/startups
- YC founder Slack communities
- Twitter replies under "AI costs are insane" or "monthly SaaS bill" posts.

## Exact Acquisition Plan for First 100 Users
1. **Direct Cold DM on Twitter**: Identify founders tweeting about their runway or AI API bills. Reply with a personalized, non-salesy message offering a free stack audit to catch overlapping subscriptions.
2. **Product Hunt Launch**: Launch as a free, useful micro-tool for founders. Title: "AI Spend Audit - Find out if your engineers are overpaying for AI".
3. **Value-Add in Communities**: Post in founder Slack channels offering free audits (e.g., "I realized I was overpaying $300/mo on redundant AI seats. Built a quick calculator to check your stack.").

## Real Messaging Examples
**Twitter Reply:**
"Hey [Name], saw you're shipping fast with Cursor and Claude. I built a quick calculator that audits your AI tool stack to find overlaps. It usually saves teams of your size around $100/mo. Want to try it?"

**Slack Post:**
"Hey folks, our AI tool bills were getting slightly out of hand (we were paying for Copilot AND Cursor Pro for the same devs). Built a quick 10-second audit tool that checks if you're overpaying for AI subscriptions. Try the audit here—it's free."
