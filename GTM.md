# Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy 🚀

This document outlines the acquisition strategy, target audience, and distribution channels for launching AI Spend Audit.

## 🎯 Target Audience

Our primary users fall into two distinct segments:

1. **The "Shadow IT" Manager (Mid-market / SMBs)**
   - **Who:** Operations Managers, fractional CFOs, or IT Leads at companies with 20-200 employees.
   - **Pain Point:** Employees are expensing individual ChatGPT Plus accounts. The company is losing visibility on data security and wasting money by not utilizing Team/Enterprise tiers.
   - **Motivation:** Cost reduction and centralized billing control.

2. **The Agency Owner (Digital Marketing / Dev Shops)**
   - **Who:** Founders of small agencies (5-20 employees).
   - **Pain Point:** Subscribed to every new AI tool that launches (Jasper, Midjourney, Runway, Claude, ChatGPT). High churn rate on tool utility.
   - **Motivation:** Trimming the fat to increase profit margins.

---

## 🏗️ The "Wedge" Strategy

We employ a **Product-Led Growth (PLG)** motion. The web application itself is the marketing tool.

1. **Immediate Value (The Hook):** The user arrives at the site and can instantly use the dynamic form to input their stack. No signup required.
2. **The "Aha" Moment:** The local logic engine instantly displays a large dollar amount (e.g., "You can save $2,400/year").
3. **The Ask (Lead Capture):** To see *exactly* which tools to cut or downgrade, the user must enter their email.
4. **Viral Loop:** The user receives a shareable, secure link (`/audit/:id`) containing the report, which they forward to their boss/CFO, organically spreading brand awareness.

## 📢 Distribution Channels

### Phase 1: The Launch (Months 1-2)
- **Product Hunt:** Launch the free audit tool. Target the "SaaS Management" and "Productivity" tags.
- **LinkedIn / X (Twitter):** Build-in-public motion. Share anonymized data (e.g., "We audited 50 agencies and found 80% are paying for redundant LLMs. Here's the breakdown.")

### Phase 2: SEO & Content (Months 3-6)
- Programmatic SEO: Create landing pages targeting high-intent search queries.
  - *Example:* "ChatGPT Plus vs Claude Pro for Agencies"
  - *Example:* "How to manage AI tool expenses in 2026"
- Publish case studies detailing how a specific company saved $X using our audit.

### Phase 3: Outbound (Months 6+)
- Cold email fractional CFOs offering a free programmatic scan of their client's Expensify/Ramp ledgers.
